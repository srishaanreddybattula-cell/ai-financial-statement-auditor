# Demo Checklist

Use this checklist when presenting the AI Financial Statement Auditor.

## 1. Start with the purpose

> This is an SEC-grounded financial reporting risk screening tool. It organizes public SEC data and surfaces indicators that may deserve additional review.

## 2. Run a company analysis

- Enter a public-company ticker such as `AAPL`.
- Select **Analyze company**.
- Show the latest annual period and data-quality status.

## 3. Explain the score

Show the overall screening score and risk dimensions. Explain that the score is a prototype screening measure, not an audit opinion or proof of wrongdoing.

## 4. Show the evidence

Open the key findings and financial signals. Point out that the application presents supporting evidence instead of only giving a score.

## 5. Show SEC provenance

Open the SEC provenance/source-filing area. Show the filing form, filing date, accession number, period end, and direct SEC.gov source filing link.

## 6. Show peer comparison

Explain that selected metrics can be compared with configured peers using metric-specific risk direction and robust deviation measures.

## 7. Show reports

Download the PDF and Excel reports. Open the source-filing link in the report to demonstrate traceability.

## 8. End with the limitation

> The system is a screening and research prototype. A flagged signal does not establish fraud, an accounting error, or a material misstatement. The underlying SEC filing should be reviewed before drawing conclusions.

## Short portfolio description

Built an SEC-grounded financial statement risk screening platform in Python and Streamlit, combining SEC XBRL data extraction, annual normalization, financial risk signals, peer comparison, accounting-policy review, filing provenance, automated tests, and PDF/Excel reporting.
